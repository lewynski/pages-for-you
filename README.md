# pages-for-you — ZHIA.OS ♥

A pixel-pink interactive desktop workspace made for **Zhia**, complete with animated roaming pixel pets, secret quotes, a 4-clue puzzle, and a locked secret confession sanctuary.

## Features
- **Pixel Pink Desktop OS (`ZHIA.OS`)** with 3 switchable pink themes (*Sakura*, *Berry Milk*, *Starry Pink*), falling pixel sakura petals, draggable/minimizable windows, and 8-bit Web Audio sound effects & music box BGM.
- **Interactive Roaming Pixel Pets**: **Mochi** (Sakura Cat), **Boba** (Pink Bunny), and **Pudding** (Pixel Chick) roam the workspace, react to petting with hearts & speech bubbles, and chase dropped treats.
- **Secret Quotes & Cozy Widgets**: Pull quotes from the **Quote Jar**, water the **Sakura Sprout**, and flip the **Sticky Note** for hidden messages.
- **4-Clue Mystery Puzzle (`Z - H - I - A`)**:
  1. **Quote Jar** — Pull 3 capsules to reveal the Golden Capsule (`Z`).
  2. **Desktop Pets / Pet Corner** — Pet or feed Mochi, Boba, or Pudding to 3 ♥ hearts (`H`).
  3. **Secret Diary** — Turn on the **Pink UV Lamp** to illuminate invisible ink (`I`).
  4. **Melody Box** — Play the 4 glowing pink piano notes (`C → E → G → B`) (`A`).
- **Secret Confession Sanctuary (`For Zhia 🔒`)**: Enter `ZHIA` to unlock an animated pixel envelope, fireworks, and the heartfelt confession letter.

## Personalize Before Publishing
Open `config.js` to customize:
- `secretQuotes` — Add your own inside jokes or sweet quotes.
- `confession` — Edit the letter paragraphs and your signature.

## Publish to GitHub Pages
1. Upload all files (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `config.js`, `audio.js`, `pets.js`, `app.js`, `README.md`) to the root of your **`pages-for-you`** GitHub repository.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages** in your repository.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch** → `main` → `/ (root)` and click **Save**.
4. Your site will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/pages-for-you/`!
